Frequently asked questions

Everything clients usually ask before their first order.

What is a Business Manager account, and why does the message limit matter?+

A Business Manager (BM) is the account structure Meta uses to manage ad accounts, pages, and messaging. The message limit determines how many messages your connected pages can send per day, which matters for outreach-heavy or high-volume ad campaigns.

What does "full verified" mean?+

Each account goes through an identity and policy check before it's handed over, so you're not starting from an unverified or flagged state.

How fast is delivery?+

Access details are sent as soon as your order is confirmed and payment is complete — message us directly to get an exact timeline for your tier.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?+

Yes — clients are served worldwide over WhatsApp, Telegram, and email, in addition to local clients in Bangladesh.

What if I need a higher limit than 10,000?+

Message us directly to discuss custom or agency-scale requirements beyond the listed tiers.
